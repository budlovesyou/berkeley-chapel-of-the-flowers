SMS Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers SMS Program for direct Chapel and venue communications.

Information we collect and use

We collect participating recipients’ mobile numbers, consent records, message content, delivery information, and related request details. We use this information only to operate, secure, and improve Chapel communications and to respond to recipients.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text message services solely to deliver the program.

Retention and choices

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for event coordination, recordkeeping, security, and legal obligations. Reply STOP to stop messages or HELP for assistance.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with the recipient’s requests and Chapel activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Contact

Questions may be sent to berkeleychapeloftheflowers@gmail.com or by replying HELP to (510) 288-2902. See the SMS Terms & Conditions.
