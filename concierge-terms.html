Chapel Concierge Terms of Use

Updated October 29, 2026. Chapel Concierge supports authorized operators of Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers with venue inquiries, correspondence and event coordination.

Authorized operation

Only the account owner or an authorized Chapel operator may connect accounts or direct actions through the concierge. Operators should review the intended recipient, message, event and date before confirming an action. The tool does not offer public account registration.

Event decisions and communications

A request, availability answer or draft is not an event agreement or guarantee of availability. Pricing, approval, payment and rental agreements remain separate. Supported booking, cancellation and email-delivery actions require review and explicit confirmation. Each authorized Chapel teammate may request and confirm a cancellation from their own phone. Unsupported events, including recurring or unlinked calendar entries, require manual review.

Delivery and availability information can change, and external services can fail. Operators should use the underlying Gmail, Calendar and Airtable records to resolve uncertainty before making commitments.

Google access and privacy

Google authorization permits the stated Gmail and Calendar functions. It does not turn on optional AI features, expand the notification audience or authorize an event decision. The account owner can revoke access through Google Account connections.

The Chapel Concierge Privacy Policy describes data handling. Internal text messaging is also governed by the existing SMS Terms & Conditions.
