Chapel Concierge Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026. This notice covers Chapel Concierge, the operations tool used by Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers. The separate SMS privacy policy also applies to the internal SMS program.

Google data accessed and why

With the Chapel account owner’s authorization, Concierge accesses Gmail sender and recipient details, subjects, message bodies, message and thread identifiers, labels and drafts to organize inquiries and prepare or deliver team-requested replies. Google Voice texts and voicemail transcripts forwarded into that mailbox can also be processed as inquiries or follow-ups.

Concierge accesses Google Calendar event details, dates and times to check availability. Its event permission supports creating confirmed reservations and canceling an identified reservation after explicit operator confirmation. The application restricts calendar writes to the configured Chapel calendar, although Google’s permission screen describes access across calendars.

Use, storage and sharing

Inquiry contact details, event requirements, conversation logs, draft responses and workflow status may be stored in the Chapel’s Airtable workspace. Processing identifiers, pending approvals, message queues and delivery records are stored on the Chapel’s server. Gmail messages and calendar events remain in the authorized Google account.

Authorized Chapel operators use this information for venue coordination. Google provides mailbox and calendar services; Airtable provides workflow records; the server hosting provider runs the concierge; and Twilio carries operational SMS and delivery information. Information is sent to these providers as needed for those functions. When an operator explicitly requests a supported email send, the message content is sent to the reviewed recipient.

Optional AI features use OpenAI to interpret requests and prepare responses from relevant inquiry, conversation and calendar context. AI processing is disabled in the current pilot. The current pilot also limits operational notifications to its designated operator. OAuth publication does not enable AI or expand those recipients.

Concierge uses Google data for its venue-coordination features, not to sell data, target advertisements or train a generalized AI model. Its use and transfer of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Protection and retention

Production Google credentials are stored encrypted on the server. Access to the concierge and its records is restricted to authorized accounts and operators. Operational records and backups are retained for coordination, troubleshooting and recordkeeping; this release does not impose an automatic deletion period.

Choices and deletion requests

The Google account owner can revoke Chapel Concierge’s Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized Google access but does not automatically erase existing Airtable records, server records or backups. Contact berkeleychapeloftheflowers@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of information retained by the Chapel. The team will review the request and any recordkeeping obligations.
