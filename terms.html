SMS Terms & Conditions

Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers uses a low-volume text messaging program for direct Chapel and venue communications with Aundre Speciale in her capacity as proprietor. Messages may cover event inquiries, availability, scheduling, tours, reservations, customer care, follow-up questions, reminders, venue logistics, and coordination with organizers, vendors, community partners, and authorized team members. We may also send occasional Chapel updates requested by the recipient.

Opting in

Recipients begin by reviewing these Terms & Conditions and texting JOIN to (510) 288-2902. We reply with the required disclosures and ask them to reply START. They are not enrolled until they reply START. People who text the Chapel first may receive one-to-one replies about that request, but they must complete this confirmation before receiving later proactive updates. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any service, and we do not use purchased or shared contact lists.
