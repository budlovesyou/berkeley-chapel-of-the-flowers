SMS Terms

Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers uses a low-volume text messaging program for direct Chapel and venue communications with Aundre Speciale in her capacity as proprietor. Messages may cover event inquiries, availability, scheduling, tours, reservations, customer care, follow-up questions, reminders, venue logistics, and coordination with organizers, vendors, community partners, and authorized team members. We may also send occasional Chapel updates requested by the recipient.

Opting in

Recipients opt in by reviewing these terms and texting START to (510) 288-2902. People who text the Chapel first may receive one-to-one replies about that request, but they must text START before receiving later proactive updates. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any service, and we do not use purchased or shared contact lists.

Confirmation message

After opting in, recipients receive: “Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers: You’re subscribed for requested Chapel and venue messages. Message frequency varies, up to 100/month. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Terms: https://chapeloftheflowers.love/sms.html Privacy: https://chapeloftheflowers.love/privacy.html”

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with the recipient’s requests and Chapel activity and may be up to 100 messages per month during active coordination. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Stopping or getting help

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help or email berkeleychapeloftheflowers@gmail.com.
