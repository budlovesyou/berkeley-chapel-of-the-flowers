Chapel text messages

To receive recurring Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers texts about your request, event coordination, scheduling, reminders, and requested Chapel updates, text START to (510) 288-2902.

Message frequency varies and may be up to 100 messages per month during active coordination. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.

By texting START, you agree to the SMS Terms & Conditions and acknowledge the SMS Privacy Policy.

Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers does not use purchased or shared contact lists.
