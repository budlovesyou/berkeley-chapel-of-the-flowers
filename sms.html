SMS Terms

Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers uses a low-volume text messaging program for authorized venue coordinators. Messages may summarize event inquiries, calendar availability, follow-up questions, human approval decisions, and other event-coordination updates.

Opting in

Authorized coordinators opt in by providing their mobile number directly to the Chapel administrator, reviewing these terms, and texting START to (415) 223-5455. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any service. This number is not a public event-booking line.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with event activity and is typically 0–20 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Stopping or getting help

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help, email berkeleychapeloftheflowers@gmail.com, or call (510) 431-2333.
