Chapel team text messages

This internal SMS program helps authorized Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers team members manage venue inquiries, calendar availability, booking decisions, reminders, customer follow-up, and event logistics. Authorized team members can enroll by texting START to (510) 288-2902.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.
