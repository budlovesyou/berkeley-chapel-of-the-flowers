Chapel Concierge

Chapel Concierge is an operations tool for the authorized team at Berkeley Chapel of the Flowers. It helps the team organize venue inquiries, review calendar availability, prepare replies and manage explicitly confirmed event reservations.

How it uses Google

The Chapel account authorizes access to its Gmail and Google Calendar. Concierge reads incoming inquiry emails, organizes messages with labels, and supports drafts and explicitly requested email replies. Calendar access supports availability checks and the team’s reservation controls.

Customers do not need to connect a Google account. The tool is for the Chapel’s own operations; this page does not offer public account registration.

Chapel Concierge is in service for the authorized Chapel team. AI helps interpret requests and prepare replies. Customer emails, bookings and cancellations require human review and explicit confirmation.

Privacy and use

Read the Chapel Concierge Privacy Policy for the data accessed, used, stored and shared, and the Chapel Concierge Terms of Use for operational boundaries.
